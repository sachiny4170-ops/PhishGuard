URL INTELLIGENCE / RULE-BASED

URL risk analyzer

Inspect a link for common phishing indicators without opening the destination.

Use a URL you are authorized to assess. Do not enter passwords or sensitive information.

What gets checked?

  • HTTPS scheme and URL structure
  • IP-address hostnames and unusual ports
  • Misleading @ symbols and punycode
  • Known URL shortener domains
  • Suspicious words and unusually long URLs

Safe handling

The analyzer parses the text locally. It does not resolve DNS, follow redirects, download content or test the remote server.

For production use, add an approved reputation API, server-side rate limiting, privacy controls and analyst review.

LEARNING CENTER / FIELD GUIDE

Recognize the manipulation.

Phishing is a social-engineering attack that tries to make people reveal information, transfer money or open a harmful link.

01 / SIGNAL
⏱

Artificial urgency

Messages claim your account will be blocked or a payment will fail unless you act immediately.

Action: Pause and contact the organization using its official app or known number.
02 / IDENTITY
♙

Sender impersonation

Attackers use lookalike domains, display-name tricks or compromised accounts.

Action: Inspect the full sender address, not only the display name.
03 / LINK
↗

Deceptive links

A link's visible text may not match its destination. Shorteners can hide the final URL.

Action: Navigate to the official website yourself instead of using the message link.
04 / REQUEST
▣

Credential harvesting

Fake login pages ask for passwords, OTPs or recovery codes.

Action: Never share OTPs. Use MFA and report suspicious login prompts.
05 / ATTACHMENT
⌑

Unexpected files

Unexpected invoices, archives or documents may contain malicious content.

Action: Verify with the sender through a separate trusted channel before opening.
06 / RESPONSE
⚑

Report, don't engage

Do not reply, click, download or forward suspicious content widely.

Action: Use your organization's phishing-report process and preserve evidence.
